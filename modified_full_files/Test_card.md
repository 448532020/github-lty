# Test Cards for Hypotheses

## Test Card 1: Fast-Fashion Impact Awareness

### Hypothesis
Displaying per-item environmental metrics (water, carbon) on product pages will reduce purchase intent among eco-conscious shoppers.
> “Seeing the water badge made me hesitate – I didn’t realize how much water goes into making this shirt.” – User A

### Experiment Design
Randomly assign 200 users to Version A (badges displayed) or Version B (no badges); survey purchase intent before and after browsing.

### Metric
Change in purchase intent rate = (Intent_after − Intent_before) in percentage points.

### Success Criteria
- Statistically significant negative change (p < 0.05)
- Effect size ≥ 5%

### Risks & Mitigations
- **Risk:** Users misunderstand badge units.  
  **Mitigation:** Add tooltip “Water saved vs. new production” on hover.

## Test Card 4: Clothing Pickup Service Adoption

### Hypothesis
At least 20% of users who visit the Pickup page will schedule an at-home pickup within 10 days.
> “I like the idea of recycling my old clothes, but I wasn’t sure how the pickup works.” – Participant 7

### Experiment Design
Track 500 Pickup page views; record “Schedule Pickup” submissions; conduct follow-up interviews with 10 users to identify friction.

### Metric
**Scheduling Rate** = Pickups_scheduled ÷ Pickup_page_views

### Success Criteria
- Scheduling rate ≥ 20% within 10 days
- ≥ 80% of interviewed users rate the scheduler as easy to use

### Risks & Mitigations
- **Risk:** Form complexity leads to drop-off.  
  **Mitigation:** Simplify form fields and add a progress bar.
