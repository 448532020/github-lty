## Learning Card 1: Quick-Action Button Engagement

**Hypothesis**
60% of new sign-ups will press a quick-action within 5 days.

**Observation**
55% clicked; median time = 60 h.
> “I tapped the button, but sometimes it didn’t register on mobile.” – Participant 8

**Findings**
- Primary issue: buttons too small.
- Secondary issue: label clarity inconsistent across screen sizes.

**Priority Actions**
1. Increase tap area by 20%.
2. Rename label to “Shop Deals.”

**Secondary Actions**
- Review responsive design for smaller devices.

**Next Steps**
A/B test “Shop Deals” vs. “View Sales” on 100 new sign-ups.
