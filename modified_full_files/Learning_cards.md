## Learning Card 1: Badge Impact on Purchase Intent

**Hypothesis**
Badges showing water & carbon data will lower purchase intent for eco-aware users.

**Observation**
15% drop in intent; “I felt guilty when I saw the water number” – Participant 3.

**Key Takeaways**
1. Water stats resonate more deeply than carbon metrics.
2. Overly technical descriptions deter engagement.

**Actions & Next Steps**
1. Highlight the water badge prominently on listings.
2. Replace carbon figures with relatable equivalents (“Equivalent to a 20 km drive”).
3. Add tooltip link “What do these numbers mean?”.
4. A/B test simplified labels to target a 10% uplift in clicks.
