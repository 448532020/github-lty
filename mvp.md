# Minimum Viable Product (Initial Version)

## In Scope

- **Home Dashboard**  
  - **Personalized Impact Metrics**: real-time display of  
    - Water saved  
    - Carbon emissions reduced  
    - Items recycled  
  - **Composite Sustainability Score**: single number summarizing overall impact  
  - **Interactive Charts**: toggle daily/weekly/monthly trends for each metric  
  - **Quick-Action Buttons**:  
    - Schedule a pickup  
    - Find a drop-off location  
    - Browse current sales  

- **Sales Catalog & Cart**  
  - **Sales Page**  
    - Filter controls: gender, size, color, style, price range  
    - Search bar with autocomplete  
    - Product cards: image carousel, brief description, rental vs. purchase toggle, price  
  - **Product Detail View**  
    - High-resolution image gallery  
    - Full description, material details, environmental footprint badge  
    - Availability calendar for rentals  
    - “Add to Cart” button  
  - **Cart & Checkout**  
    - Cart summary: editable quantities, remove items  
    - Checkout form: contact info, shipping address, order summary  
    - Payment integration placeholder (e.g. Stripe test mode)  
    - Order confirmation page and email notification  

- **Recycling Drop-off Map**  
  - **Interactive Map**  
    - Autocomplete search for address or landmark  
    - Radius filter slider (e.g. 5 km / 10 km)  
    - Map pins showing drop-off points (charities, clothing banks, repair shops)  
  - **Location Details**  
    - Name, address, opening hours  
    - Accepted item list  
    - Directions link (Google Maps)  

- **Pickup Scheduler**  
  - **Booking Calendar**  
    - Flatpickr-powered date/time selector with available slots  
  - **Pickup Form**  
    - Pickup address (pre-filled from profile)  
    - Item list uploader (text list or photos)  
    - Special instructions field  
  - **Confirmation & Reminders**  
    - Summary screen with “Edit” option  
    - Automated reminder (email/SMS) 24 hours before  

- **Profile & Account Hub**  
  - **Profile Management**  
    - View/edit: name, email, phone, postal address  
    - Change password workflow  
    - Upload/change avatar  
  - **My Rentals**  
    - List of active and past rentals with:  
      - Item image & title  
      - Rental period and return due date  
      - “Initiate Return” button for active rentals  
  - **My Recycling**  
    - Recycle history log:  
      - Item photos  
      - Date and method (drop-off vs. pickup)  
      - Points earned per entry  
  - **Rewards Dashboard**  
    - Points balance meter  
    - Available coupons/discount cards with “Redeem” buttons  
    - Redemption history  
  - **Help & Support**  
    - FAQ section (accordion layout)  
    - Contact form: subject dropdown, message textarea  
    - Live-chat placeholder for future integration
