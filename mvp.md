# Minimum Viable Product (Concise Version)

## In Scope

### 1. Home Dashboard
- **Impact Metrics**: Displays total water saved, carbon reduced, and items recycled with weekly/monthly trends.
- **Composite Score**: Simple gauge summarizing overall sustainability impact.
- **Quick Actions**:  
  - Schedule a pickup  
  - Find a drop-off location  
  - Browse current sales

### 2. Sales Catalog & Cart
- **Sales Page**:  
  - Filters (gender, size, price, condition)  
  - Product cards show image, price, and environmental badge  
- **Product Detail**:  
  - Photos, description, footprint badge, “Add to Cart” button  
- **Cart & Checkout**:  
  - Item list with quantity/edit options  
  - Two-step checkout (shipping → payment)

### 3. Recycling Drop-off Map
- **Interactive Map**:  
  - Search by address or radius (5/10/20 km)  
  - Markers for charity bins and recycling centers  
- **Location Details**:  
  - Name, address, opening hours, accepted items, “Get Directions”

### 4. Pickup Scheduler
- **Calendar Selector**:  
  - Shows available dates and time slots (enforce 2-hour lead time)  
- **Pickup Form**:  
  - Pickup address (editable), text/photo list of items, optional notes  
  - Confirmation screen with automated email/app reminder 24 hours prior

### 5. Profile & Account Hub
- **Profile Management**:  
  - View/edit personal info, change password, upload avatar  
- **My Recycling**:  
  - Log of recycled items (date, method, points) and points balance  
- **My Orders & Rewards**:  
  - Order history, available coupons, redemption history  
- **Help & Support**:  
  - FAQ section and contact form

## Out of Scope
- Rental features  
- Admin analytics dashboard  
- Third-party loyalty integrations
