# Business Model Canvas

## 1. Customer Segments
- **Environmentally-conscious Shoppers**  
  Individuals prioritizing second-hand purchases and recycling, valuing transparent impact metrics.
- **Busy Individuals**  
  Seeking streamlined recycling with automated scheduling and reminders.
- **Budget-conscious Shoppers**  
  Motivated by discounts, rewards, and accessible second-hand quality.
- **Value-driven Purchasers**  
  Interested in sustainable products with visible environmental impact.

## 2. Value Propositions
- **All-in-one Sustainability Hub**  
  Combines second-hand marketplace, recycling map, pickup scheduler, and impact dashboard—no app-switching required.  
  “I completed my first drop-off in under 2 minutes.” – Busy Parent

- **Transparent Impact Metrics**  
  Real-time water saved, carbon reduced, and Composite Sustainability Score (0–100).

- **Seamless Convenience**  
  Unified interface for shopping, recycling history, and rewards.

- **Key Risk**  
  Information overload; mitigate by surfacing top 3 impact stats per session.

## 3. Channels
- **Web & Mobile App**  
  Responsive design, push notifications for pickups, coupons, and flash sales.
- **Social Media & Influencer Partnerships**  
  Eco-influencer campaigns, user-generated content hashtags.
- **Email & Push Campaigns**  
  Weekly impact summaries, abandoned cart reminders, coupon alerts.
- **In-app Banners & Prompts**  
  Contextual calls to action for recycling and shopping.

## 4. Customer Relationships
- **Self-service Portal** with tooltips and guidance.
- **Automated Reminders** via email/SMS/app.
- **In-app Micro-surveys** post-purchase and post-recycle.
- **Light-touch Support**: FAQ, contact form, live-chat placeholder.

## 5. Revenue Streams
- **Sales Commissions** on second-hand sales.
- **Affiliate & Promotional Partnerships** with brands and recycling centers.
- **Future Premium Subscriptions** for early access and expedited services.

## 6. Key Activities
- Platform development & maintenance.
- Pickup coordination & logistics.
- Educational content creation.
- Impact tracking & user surveys.

## 7. Key Resources
- Technology infrastructure (hosting, mapping libraries).
- Operational assets (storage, transport).
- Human resources (volunteers, interns).
- Data & content (environmental datasets, guides).

## 8. Key Partners
- Logistics providers.
- Recycling centers & NGOs.
- Payment gateways.
- Data providers & influencers.

## 9. Cost Structure
- Technology development & maintenance.
- Operational logistics and storage.
- Educational content production.
- Packaging & transport costs.
