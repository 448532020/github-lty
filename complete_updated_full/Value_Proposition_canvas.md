# Value Proposition Canvas

## 1. Customer Profile

### Customer Jobs
- Discover & act on impact: view personal dashboards and compare metrics.
- Acquire sustainable apparel: browse curated second-hand catalog.
- Dispose of unwanted clothing: locate bins or book pickups.
- Manage account & rewards: track orders, recycling history, and points.

### Pains
- Complex recycling options and unclear map icons.
- Uncertainty about actual environmental impact.
- Checkout friction with multi-step forms.
- Profile setup friction and file upload confusion.
- Low visibility of rewards and coupon expirations.

### Gains
- Instant eco-feedback on water and carbon savings.
- One-stop shopping, recycling, and scheduling.
- Flexible drop-off vs. pickup choices.
- Streamlined guest-checkout with autocomplete.
- Gamified rewards and badges.

## 2. Value Map

### Products & Services
1. **Home Dashboard**: Quick-actions, impact charts, sustainability score gauge.
2. **Sales Catalog & Cart**: Filters, footprints, two-step checkout.
3. **Recycling Map**: Interactive pins, clustering, list view.
4. **Pickup Scheduler**: Flatpickr calendar, grouped slots, reminder system.
5. **Profile Hub**: Personal info, recycling log, order history, support.

### Barriers Addressed
- Difficulty finding drop-off points and scheduling pickups.  
  *Solution:* Unified map + scheduler; “Mark as Visited” feature.  
  “I booked my first pickup in under 2 minutes.” – Busy Parent

### Top Gains
- Immediate view of water saved and carbon avoided after actions.
- Guest-checkout removes friction for new users.
- Automated reminders boost engagement.
