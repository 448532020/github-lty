# Learning Cards for Hypotheses

## Learning Card 1: Environmental Footprint Badge Awareness

### Hypothesis
Displaying badges with water & carbon data will lower purchase intent among eco-aware users.
> “I felt guilty when I saw the water number.” – Participant 1

### Observation
15% drop in purchase intent for users with footprint badges.

### Key Takeaways
1. Water metrics resonate more than carbon figures.  
2. Technical tooltips can overwhelm users.

### Actions & Next Steps
1. Highlight water badge on listings.  
2. Simplify carbon tooltip (“Equivalent to driving 15 km”).  
3. Add “What does this mean?” info link.  
4. A/B test simplified labels for 10% click uplift.

## Learning Card 2: Sales Catalog Conversion

### Hypothesis
12% of “Add to Cart” actions will convert to purchase in the same session.
> “I found the checkout process too long.” – Participant 2

### Observation
10% conversion; 25% drop-off at payment.

### Key Takeaways
1. Progress indicators help, but forms remain confusing.  
2. Guest-checkout needs clearer callouts.

### Actions & Next Steps
1. Add dynamic progress bar.  
2. Clarify guest-checkout tooltip.  
3. Auto-detect city from ZIP.

## Learning Card 3: Recycling Drop-off Map Interaction

### Hypothesis
30% of map searches will lead to detail-view clicks.
> “Clusters made me miss locations.” – Participant 3

### Observation
28% detail-view rate; cluster confusion noted.

### Key Takeaways
1. Pins too small; clustering thresholds need tuning.  
2. Legend needs clearer labels.

### Actions & Next Steps
1. Increase pin size by 20%.  
2. Add “Zoom in to see all” hint.  
3. A/B test updated legend design.

## Learning Card 4: Pickup Scheduler Adoption

### Hypothesis
20% of Pickup page visitors will schedule a pickup within 10 days.
> “I wasn’t sure if times were AM or PM.” – Participant 4

### Observation
18% scheduling rate; slot clarity issues.

### Key Takeaways
1. Time grouping under Morning/Afternoon required.  
2. “Next Available” banner needs emphasis.

### Actions & Next Steps
1. Implement collapsible slot groups.  
2. Highlight “Next Available” slot.  
3. Tooltip: “Slots open 48 hours in advance.”
