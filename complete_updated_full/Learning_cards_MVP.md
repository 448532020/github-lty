# Learning Cards for MVP Hypotheses

## Learning Card 1: Home Dashboard Engagement

### Hypothesis
60% of new users will click a quick-action within 5 days.
> “I tapped the button but it sometimes didn’t register.” – Participant 5

### Observation
55% clicked; median time = 60 hours.

### Findings
- Buttons too small for mobile.  
- Labels unclear on smaller screens.

### Priority Actions
1. Increase tap area by 20%.  
2. Rename label to “Shop Deals.”

### Next Steps
A/B test “Shop Deals” vs. “View Sales” on 100 users.

---

## Learning Card 2: Sales & Checkout Conversion

### Hypothesis
12% of “Add to Cart” actions convert to purchase in the same session.
> “I missed the first-order discount prompt.” – Participant 6

### Observation
9% conversion; 30% drop-off at payment.

### Findings
- Lack of first-order discount visible early.  
- Payment form felt lengthy.

### Priority Actions
1. Display “10% off first order” coupon prominently.  
2. Simplify payment form fields.

### Next Steps
Measure conversion uplift over 2 weeks.

---

## Learning Card 3: Pickup & Drop-off Flow Adoption

### Hypothesis
25% of page visitors will schedule or view details within 10 days.
> “I clicked the icon but didn’t see confirmation.” – Participant 7

### Observation
18% pickup adoption; 22% detail-view rate.

### Findings
- Iconography confusing.  
- “Next Available” hint too subtle.

### Priority Actions
1. Update icon labels with text.  
2. Emphasize “Next Available” banner.

### Next Steps
Collect qualitative feedback post-update.

---

## Learning Card 4: Profile Completion & Rewards Redemption

### Hypothesis
75% profile completion within 48h; 18% coupon redemption within 30 days.
> “I didn’t know I had a coupon to apply.” – Participant 8

### Observation
80% profile completion; 15% redemption rate.

### Findings
- Email delays affect coupon awareness.  
- Manual code entry deters redemption.

### Priority Actions
1. SMS fallback for email verification.  
2. Auto-apply valid coupons at checkout.

### Next Steps
Track redemption rate changes next month.
