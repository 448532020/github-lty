## Test Card 1: Environmental Footprint Badge Awareness

**HYPOTHESIS**  
Displaying per-item environmental footprint badges (water & carbon metrics) on product detail pages will reduce purchase intent among eco-conscious shoppers.

**TEST PLAN**  
Randomly assign 200 users to see the footprint badge on product pages versus a control group without it; survey purchase intent before and after browsing.

**METRIC**  
- **Purchase-intent change:**  
  (% intending to buy after exposure) − (% intending to buy before exposure)

**CRITERIA**  
Statistically significant negative change in purchase intent for users exposed to footprint badges (p < 0.05).

## Test Card 2: Sales Catalog Conversion

**HYPOTHESIS**  
At least 12% of users who add an item to their cart will complete checkout in the same session under the streamlined two-step checkout flow.

**TEST PLAN**  
Track “Add to Cart,” “Begin Checkout,” and “Order Completed” events for the first 1,000 add-to-cart actions; conduct 10 usability interviews focusing on checkout friction.

**METRIC**  
- **Conversion rate:**  
  (Completed orders) ÷ (Add-to-cart actions)

**CRITERIA**  
Conversion rate ≥ 12% **and** drop-off between “Begin Checkout” and payment ≤ 20%.

## Test Card 3: Recycling Drop-off Map Interaction

**HYPOTHESIS**  
Providing an interactive drop-off map with clear custom markers will lead at least 30% of map searches to result in a detail-view click.

**TEST PLAN**  
Track “Search” inputs and “View Details” clicks on the Drop-off page for the first 500 searches; interview 15 users about map usability.

**METRIC**  
- **Detail-view rate:**  
  (Detail-view clicks) ÷ (Map searches)

**CRITERIA**  
Detail-view rate ≥ 30% and users report ease of locating nearby bins in interviews.

## Test Card 4: Pickup Scheduler Adoption

**HYPOTHESIS**  
At least 20% of users who visit the Pickup page will schedule an at-home pickup slot within 10 days of first viewing.

**TEST PLAN**  
Track “Pickup Page View” and “Confirm Pickup” submissions for the first 500 page views; survey 20 users on scheduling barriers.

**METRIC**  
- **Scheduling rate:**  
  (Pickups scheduled) ÷ (Pickup page views)

**CRITERIA**  
Scheduling rate ≥ 20% **and** median time-to-schedule ≤ 5 days.
