
## Test Cards

1. **Fast-Fashion Impact Awareness**  
   **Hypothesis**  
   Displaying per-item environmental metrics (water, carbon) on product pages will reduce purchase intent among eco-conscious shoppers.  
   **Test Plan**  
   - Randomly assign 200 users to either see the environmental badge on product pages or not.  
   - Survey each user’s purchase intent both before and after browsing.  
   **Metric**  
   Change in purchase-intent rate =  
   (% of users intending to buy after exposure) −  
   (% of users intending to buy before exposure).  
   **Success**  
   A statistically significant negative change in intent for the metric group.

2. **Sales Catalog Conversion**  
   **Hypothesis**  
   At least **12%** of “Add to Cart” actions will convert into completed orders in the same session.  
   **Test Plan**  
   - Track “Add to Cart,” “Begin Checkout,” and “Order Completed” events for the first 1,000 add-to-cart actions.  
   - Run 10 usability interviews to identify friction points.  
   **Metric**  
   Conversion rate = (Number of completed orders) ÷ (Number of “Add to Cart” actions).  
   **Success**  
   ≥ 12% conversion and ≤ 20% drop-off between “Begin Checkout” and payment.

3. **Recycling Drop-off Map Usage**  
   **Hypothesis**  
   Making nearby recycling points easy to find on a map will drive at least **30%** of map searches into detail-view clicks.  
   **Test Plan**  
   - Instrument “Search” and “View Details” clicks on the Drop-off page for the first 500 searches.  
   - Collect brief feedback from 15 users on map usability.  
   **Metric**  
   Detail-view rate = (Number of detail-view clicks) ÷ (Number of searches).  
   **Success**  
   ≥ 30% detail-view rate.

4. **Clothing Pickup Service Adoption**  
   **Hypothesis**  
   At least **20%** of users who visit the Pickup page will schedule an at-home pickup within 10 days.  
   **Test Plan**  
   - Track page views and “Schedule Pickup” submissions for the first 500 visits.  
   - Survey 20 users about any barriers.  
   **Metric**  
   Scheduling rate = (Number of pickups scheduled) ÷ (Pickup page views).  
   **Success**  
   ≥ 20% scheduling rate.
