## Test Cards

1. **Fast‐Fashion Impact Awareness**  
   **Hypothesis**  
   Showing per‐item environmental metrics (water, carbon) on the product page will reduce traditional “fast‐fashion” purchase intent among eco‐conscious shoppers.  
   **Test Plan**  
   - Randomly assign 200 users to either see the environmental metrics badge on product pages or not.  
   - Survey stated purchase intent before and after browsing.  
   **Metric**  
   Δ in % of users who say they intend to buy.  
   **Success**  
   Statistically significant drop in purchase intent for the metric group.

2. **Sales Catalog Conversion**  
   **Hypothesis**  
   At least **12%** of users who add an item to cart will complete checkout in the same session.  
   **Test Plan**  
   - Track “Add to Cart,” “Begin Checkout,” and “Order Completed” events for the first **1,000** add‐to‐cart actions.  
   - Conduct 10 usability interviews to surface friction.  
   **Metric**  
   Conversion rate = completed orders ÷ add‐to‐cart events.  
   **Success**  
   ≥ 12% conversion and ≤ 20% drop-off between checkout start and payment.

3. **Recycling Drop‐off Map Usage**  
   **Hypothesis**  
   Making nearby recycling points easy to find on a map will drive at least **30%** of map searches into detail‐view clicks.  
   **Test Plan**  
   - Instrument “Search” and “View Details” clicks on the Drop-off page for the first **500** searches.  
   - Collect brief feedback from 15 users on map usability.  
   **Metric**  
   Detail‐view rate = detail clicks ÷ searches.  
   **Success**  
   ≥ 30% detail‐view rate.

4. **Clothing Pickup Service Adoption**  
   **Hypothesis**  
   At least **20%** of users who visit the Pickup page will schedule an at‐home pickup within **10 days**.  
   **Test Plan**  
   - Track page views and “Schedule Pickup” submissions for the first **500** visits.  
   - Survey a subset (n=20) about any signup barriers.  
   **Metric**  
   Adoption rate = pickups scheduled ÷ page views.  
   **Success**  
   ≥ 20% scheduling rate.
