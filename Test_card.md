# Test Cards for Your Hypotheses

## Test Card 1: Fast-Fashion Impact Awareness  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
Displaying per-item environmental metrics (water, carbon) on product pages will reduce purchase intent among eco-conscious shoppers.

**TEST PLAN**  
Randomly assign 200 users to see the environmental badge on product pages or not; survey purchase intent before and after browsing.

**METRIC**  
Change in purchase-intent rate =  
(% intending to buy after exposure) − (% intending to buy before exposure).

**CRITERIA**  
Statistically significant negative change in intent for the group seeing the metrics.

## Test Card 2: Sales Catalog Conversion  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
At least 12% of users who add an item to cart will complete checkout in the same session.

**TEST PLAN**  
Track “Add to Cart,” “Begin Checkout,” and “Order Completed” events for the first 1,000 add-to-cart actions; conduct 10 usability interviews.

**METRIC**  
Conversion rate = (Completed orders) ÷ (Add-to-cart actions).

**CRITERIA**  
Conversion ≥ 12% **and** drop-off between “Begin Checkout” and payment ≤ 20%.

## Test Card 3: Recycling Drop-off Map Usage  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
Making nearby recycling points easy to find on a map will drive at least 30% of map searches into detail-view clicks.

**TEST PLAN**  
Instrument “Search” and “View Details” clicks on the Drop-off page for the first 500 searches; gather feedback from 15 users.

**METRIC**  
Detail-view rate = (Detail-view clicks) ÷ (Searches).

**CRITERIA**  
Detail-view rate ≥ 30%.

## Test Card 4: Clothing Pickup Service Adoption  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
At least 20% of users who visit the Pickup page will schedule an at-home pickup within 10 days.

**TEST PLAN**  
Track page views and “Schedule Pickup” submissions for the first 500 visits; survey 20 users on barriers.

**METRIC**  
Scheduling rate = (Pickups scheduled) ÷ (Pickup page views).

**CRITERIA**  
Scheduling rate ≥ 20%.
