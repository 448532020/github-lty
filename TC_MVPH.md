# Test Cards for Your Minimum Viable Product Hypotheses

## Test Card 1: Home Dashboard Engagement  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
At least 60% of new users will click one of the Home Dashboard quick-action buttons (“Schedule a Pickup,” “Find a Drop-off Location,” or “Browse Sales”) within their first 5 days.

**TEST PLAN**  
Roll out the Home Dashboard to the first 200 registered users; instrument analytics to capture each quick-action click and measure time from signup to first click.

**METRIC**  
- **Engagement rate** = (Users with ≥ 1 quick-action click) ÷ 200  
- **Median time to first click** in hours

**CRITERIA**  
Success if:  
- Engagement rate ≥ 60%  
- Median time to first click ≤ 48 hours

## Test Card 2: Sales & Checkout Conversion  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
At least 12% of “Add to Cart” actions will convert to a completed order within the same session under the new checkout flow.

**TEST PLAN**  
Track “Add to Cart,” “Begin Checkout,” and “Order Completed” events for the first 1,000 add-to-cart actions; conduct usability interviews with 10 participants focusing on checkout clarity.

**METRIC**  
- **Conversion rate** = (Completed orders) ÷ (Add-to-cart actions)  
- **Drop-off rate** between “Begin Checkout” and payment step

**CRITERIA**  
Success if:  
- Conversion rate ≥ 12%  
- Drop-off between “Begin Checkout” and payment ≤ 20%

---

## Test Card 3: Pickup & Drop-off Flow Adoption  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
At least 25% of users who visit the Pickup or Drop-off pages will schedule a pickup or view a location’s details within 10 days of first visit.

**TEST PLAN**  
- Instrument “Pickup Page View” and “Confirm Pickup” events  
- Instrument “Drop-off Page Search” and “View Location Details” events  
- Survey 20 users on flow clarity and ease of use

**METRIC**  
- **Pickup adoption rate** = (Pickups scheduled) ÷ (Pickup page views)  
- **Drop-off detail-view rate** = (Detail-view clicks) ÷ (Drop-off page searches)

**CRITERIA**  
Success if:  
- Pickup adoption ≥ 20%  
- Drop-off detail-view rate ≥ 25%

---

## Test Card 4: Profile Completion & Rewards Redemption  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
- ≥ 75% of new users will complete their Profile (all fields filled, avatar uploaded) within 48 hours of signup.  
- ≥ 18% of users issued a coupon will redeem at least one within 30 days.

**TEST PLAN**  
- Track “Profile Completed” events for new sign-ups  
- Track “Coupon Issued” and “Coupon Redeemed” events for the first cohort of 500 users; poll 15 users on obstacles to redemption

**METRIC**  
- **Profile completion rate** = (Profiles completed within 48 h) ÷ (New sign-ups)  
- **Redemption rate** = (Coupons redeemed) ÷ (Coupons issued)

**CRITERIA**  
Success if:  
- Profile completion ≥ 75% within 48 hours  
- Redemption rate ≥ 18% within 30 days
