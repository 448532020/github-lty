# Test Cards for Minimum Viable Product Hypotheses

## 1. Home Dashboard Engagement  
**Hypothesis**  
At least 60% of new users will click one of the Home Dashboard quick-action buttons (Schedule Pickup, Find Drop-off, Browse Sales) within their first 5 days.  

**Test Plan**  
- Roll out the Dashboard to the first 200 sign-ups.  
- Instrument analytics on each quick-action button.  
- Measure time from signup to first click.  

**Metrics & Criteria**  
- Engagement rate = (# users with ≥1 click) / (200).  
- Success if engagement rate ≥ 60% and median time to first click ≤ 48 hours.

---

## 2. Sales & Checkout Conversion  
**Hypothesis**  
At least 12% of “Add to Cart” actions will convert into a completed order within the same session.  

**Test Plan**  
- Track “Add to Cart,” “Begin Checkout,” and “Order Completed” events.  
- Conduct 10 usability interviews to identify friction points.  

**Metrics & Criteria**  
- Conversion rate = completed orders / add-to-cart events.  
- Success if conversion rate ≥ 12% and drop-off from checkout start to payment ≤ 20%.

---

## 3. Pickup & Drop-off Flow Adoption  
**Hypothesis**  
At least 25% of users who visit the Pickup or Drop-off pages will schedule a pickup or view a location’s details within 10 days.  

**Test Plan**  
- Instrument page views on Pickup and Drop-off.  
- Track “Schedule Pickup” submissions and “View Location Details” clicks.  
- Survey 20 users on clarity of each flow.  

**Metrics & Criteria**  
- Pickup adoption rate = pickups scheduled / pickup page views.  
- Drop-off detail-view rate = detail views / drop-off searches.  
- Success if pickup ≥ 20% and detail-view ≥ 25%.

---

## 4. Profile Completion & Rewards Redemption  
**Hypothesis**  
- ≥ 75% of new users will complete their Profile (all fields filled, avatar uploaded) within 48 hours of signup.  
- ≥ 18% of users issued a coupon will redeem at least one within 30 days.  

**Test Plan**  
- Track “Profile Completed” and “Redeem” events.  
- Poll a sample of 15 users about any obstacles.  

**Metrics & Criteria**  
- Profile completion rate = completed profiles / new sign-ups.  
- Redemption rate = redeemed coupons / issued coupons.  
- Success if profile ≥ 75% in 48 h and redemption ≥ 18% in 30 days.
