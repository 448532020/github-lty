# Test Cards for Your Minimum Viable Product Hypotheses

## Test Card 1: Home Dashboard Engagement  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
At least 60% of new users will click one of the Home Dashboard quick-action buttons (Schedule Pickup, Find Drop-off, Browse Sales) within their first 5 days.

**TEST PLAN**  
Roll out the Dashboard to the first 200 sign-ups; instrument analytics on each quick-action button and measure time from signup to first click.

**METRIC**  
Engagement rate = (Users with ≥1 quick-action click) ÷ 200.

**CRITERIA**  
Success if engagement rate ≥ 60% **and** median time to first click ≤ 48 hours.

---

## Test Card 2: Sales & Checkout Conversion  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
At least 12% of “Add to Cart” actions will convert into a completed order within the same session.

**TEST PLAN**  
Track “Add to Cart,” “Begin Checkout,” and “Order Completed” events for the first 1,000 add-to-cart actions; conduct 10 usability interviews to surface friction points.

**METRIC**  
Conversion rate = (Completed orders) ÷ (Add-to-cart actions).

**CRITERIA**  
Success if conversion rate ≥ 12% **and** drop-off between “Begin Checkout” and payment ≤ 20%.

---

## Test Card 3: Pickup & Drop-off Flow Adoption  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
At least 25% of users who visit the Pickup or Drop-off pages will schedule a pickup or view a location’s details within 10 days.

**TEST PLAN**  
Instrument page views on Pickup and Drop-off; track “Schedule Pickup” submissions and “View Location Details” clicks; survey 20 users on flow clarity.

**METRIC**  
- Pickup adoption rate = (Pickups scheduled) ÷ (Pickup page views)  
- Detail-view rate = (Detail-view clicks) ÷ (Drop-off searches)

**CRITERIA**  
Success if pickup adoption ≥ 20% **and** detail-view rate ≥ 25%.

---

## Test Card 4: Profile Completion & Rewards Redemption  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
- ≥ 75% of new users will complete their Profile (all fields filled, avatar uploaded) within 48 hours of signup.  
- ≥ 18% of users issued a coupon will redeem at least one within 30 days.

**TEST PLAN**  
Track “Profile Completed” and “Reward Redeemed” events; poll 15 users on obstacles encountered.

**METRIC**  
- Profile completion rate = (Profiles completed) ÷ (New sign-ups)  
- Redemption rate = (Coupons redeemed) ÷ (Coupons issued)

**CRITERIA**  
Success if profile completion ≥ 75% within 48 h **and** redemption rate ≥ 18% within 30 days.
