
# Value Proposition Canvas

## Trend-focused Consumers

- **Jobs to be Done:**  
  Rent trendy clothes at an affordable price without having to buy new items constantly.

- **Pains:**  
  High cost of buying new clothes for short-term use.

- **Gains:**  
  Access to a wide range of trendy clothes at a lower cost.

- **Pain Relievers:**  
  Affordable rental prices on the resale/rental platform.

- **Gain Creators:**  
  A large selection of trendy clothes available for rent.

---

## Environmentally-conscious Shoppers

- **Jobs to be Done:**  
  Make more sustainable fashion choices and reduce their environmental impact.

- **Pains:**  
  Lack of awareness about the environmental costs of fast fashion.

- **Gains:**  
  Feeling good about making environmentally-friendly choices.

- **Pain Relievers:**  
  The fast fashion impact awareness page provides clear environmental cost data.

- **Gain Creators:**  
  Encouraging reduced fast-fashion purchases and promoting sustainable alternatives.

---

## Busy Individuals

- **Jobs to be Done:**  
  Donate clothes easily without having to find time to drop them off.

- **Pains:**  
  Forgetting to donate clothes due to a busy schedule.

- **Gains:**  
  A convenient way to contribute to clothing recycling.

- **Pain Relievers:**  
  The monthly clothing pickup service with tracking notifications.

- **Gain Creators:**  
  Peace of mind knowing that clothes are being donated regularly.

---

## Budget-conscious Shoppers

- **Jobs to be Done:**  
  Find affordable and good-quality clothing.

- **Pains:**  
  High prices of new clothes.

- **Gains:**  
  Access to a variety of second-hand clothes at low prices.

- **Pain Relievers:**  
  The thrift store website showcases a wide range of affordable options.

- **Gain Creators:**  
  A user-friendly online shopping experience for second-hand clothes.
