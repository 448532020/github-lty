# Business Model Canvas

## 1. Customer Segments  
- **Trend-focused Consumers** who want affordable access to the latest styles via rentals.  
- **Environmentally-conscious Shoppers** seeking data-driven ways to reduce their fashion footprint.  
- **Busy Individuals** needing a simple way to donate or recycle clothes without extra effort.  
- **Budget-conscious Shoppers** looking for quality second-hand apparel at low cost.  

## 2. Value Propositions  
- **All-in-one Sustainability Hub:** a single web/mobile interface combining impact metrics, rentals, sales, recycling and pickup scheduling :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}  
- **Affordable Trend Rentals:** flexible, user-set pricing on high-fashion items for short– or long-term use :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}  
- **Transparent Impact Metrics:** real-time dashboard showing water saved, carbon reduced and items recycled to guide eco-friendly choices :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}  
- **Convenient Recycling & Pickup:** interactive map of drop-off points plus a calendar-driven at-home pickup scheduler :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}  
- **Reward-Driven Engagement:** point-based coupons and gamified achievements to encourage repeat use :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}  

## 3. Channels  
- **Web & Mobile App** as the primary user interface.  
- **Social Media & Influencer Partnerships** targeting trend-focused and eco-conscious communities.  
- **Email Campaigns & Push Notifications** for reminders (pickups, new coupons, featured sales).  
- **On-platform Banners & In-app Prompts** to surface quick-actions (e.g. “Book Pickup,” “Shop Rentals”).  

## 4. Customer Relationships  
- **Self-service**: intuitive dashboard and flows for rentals, purchases, recycling and pickups.  
- **Automated Reminders** via email/SMS/app for scheduled pickups, rental returns and coupon expirations.  
- **In-app Micro-Surveys** to gather feedback on key flows (e.g. checkout, map usability).  
- **Light Touch Support**: FAQ section and contact form in the Profile & Help area.  

## 5. Revenue Streams  
- **Rental Fees** paid per item per duration (user-defined within suggested ranges) :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}  
- **Sales Commissions** on second-hand purchases in the catalog :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}  
- **Future Premium Subscriptions** for advanced analytics or priority pickups.  
- **Affiliate Fees** from partner recycling centers or curbside services.  

## 6. Key Resources  
- **Technology Stack**: front-end (React/Vue), back-end services, mapping API, notification system.  
- **Environmental Data Feeds** for accurate water & carbon metrics.  
- **Operations Team** coordinating logistics, matching rentals, and managing coupons.  
- **Analytics & A/B Testing Tools** (Mixpanel, FullStory) to instrument and optimize flows.  

## 7. Key Activities  
- **Developing & Maintaining** core features: Dashboard, Rentals, Sales, Map, Scheduler, Profile hub.  
- **Order & Request Fulfillment** for rentals, at-home pickups and drop-off coordination.  
- **Data Analysis & Experimentation** to refine engagement and conversion (Test & Learning Cards).  
- **Content & Inventory Management** for product listings, impact messaging and partner sourcing.  

## 8. Key Partners  
- **Logistics Providers** for at-home pickups/drop-offs.  
- **Recycling Centers & Charity Shops** to process donated textiles.  
- **Payment Gateway** (Stripe or similar) for checkout integration.  
- **Data Providers** supplying water-use and carbon-emission metrics.  
- **Marketing Affiliates & Influencers** to reach target segments.  

## 9. Cost Structure  
- **Platform Development & Hosting** (servers, APIs, mapping).  
- **Logistics & Operations** costs for pickups, drop-offs and inventory handling.  
- **Data Licensing** for environmental impact calculations.  
- **Marketing & Advertising** spend to acquire and engage users.  
- **Customer Support & Staffing** for operations and troubleshooting.
