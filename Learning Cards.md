## Resale/Rental Platform

**HYPOTHESIS**  
We believed that 60% of trend-focused users will rent clothes if affordable.

**OBSERVATION**  
We observed that the actual percentage of rental requests from trend-focused users was lower than 60%.

**LEARNING AND INSIGHTS**  
From that we learnt that the affordability criteria may need to be re-evaluated or other factors such as clothing variety could be influencing their decision.

**DECISIONS AND ACTIONS**  
Therefore, we will conduct more in-depth user interviews to understand the barriers to renting and consider expanding the clothing inventory.

## Fast Fashion Impact Awareness

**HYPOTHESIS**  
We believed that showing environmental costs reduces purchase intent.

**OBSERVATION**  
We observed that there was no significant decrease in purchase intent after users saw the environmental cost data.

**LEARNING AND INSIGHTS**  
From that we learnt that the presentation of environmental costs may not be impactful enough or other factors like brand loyalty may be more influential.

**DECISIONS AND ACTIONS**  
Therefore, we will improve the design and content of the awareness page and segment the audience further to target those more likely to be influenced.

## Recycling Drop-off Map

**HYPOTHESIS**  
We believed that users will recycle if options are easy to find.

**OBSERVATION**  
We observed that the number of users visiting the recommended recycling spots was lower than expected.

**LEARNING AND INSIGHTS**  
From that we learnt that the information provided may not be detailed enough or other barriers to recycling still exist.

**DECISIONS AND ACTIONS**  
Therefore, we will provide more detailed information such as opening hours and accepted materials, and partner with local recycling centers.

## Thrift Store/Op Shop Website

**HYPOTHESIS**  
We believed that showcasing used clothes on models and providing an informative page would increase purchase rate.

**OBSERVATION**  
We observed that there was no significant increase in second-hand clothes sales.
