## Learning Card 1: Quick-Action Button Engagement

**Hypothesis**  
60% of new sign-ups will press a quick-action within 5 days.

**Observation**  
55% clicked; median time = 60 h.

**Findings**  
- Buttons too small and labels unclear.  
- “Browse Sales” hidden on smaller screens.

**Actions**  
1. Increase button tap area by 20%.  
2. Rename label to “Shop Deals.”

**Next Steps**  
A/B test “Shop Deals” vs. “View Sales” on 100 new users.
