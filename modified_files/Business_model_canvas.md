### 2. Value Propositions  
- **Holistic Sustainability Hub**  
  Integrates curated second-hand sales, real-time impact metrics, and pickup scheduling—no app-switching required.  

- **Transparent Eco-Insights**  
  Live dashboards compare your water & carbon savings against local averages.  

- **Seamless Convenience**  
  One dashboard for purchases, recycling history, and rewards—keeping users engaged.  

- **Key Risk**  
  Overwhelming users with data; mitigate by surfacing only top 3 impact stats per session.
