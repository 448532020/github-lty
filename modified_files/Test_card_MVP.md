## Test Card 1: Home Dashboard Engagement Experiment

**Hypothesis**  
“New users will click a quick-action if they clearly understand its purpose.”

**Design & Method**  
Roll out the quick-action UI to 200 new sign-ups; instrument analytics for each button click; record device type.

**Primary Metric**  
- **Engagement Rate** = Users_with_≥1_click ÷ Total_users

**Potential Confounders**  
- Terminology (“Browse Sales” vs. “Shop Deals”)  
- Device type (mobile vs. desktop)

**Key Learnings to Capture**  
- Engagement differences by device  
- Feedback on label clarity
