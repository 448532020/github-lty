## Learning Card 1: Badge Impact on Purchase Intent

**Hypothesis**  
Badges showing water & carbon data will lower purchase intent for eco-aware users.

**Observation**  
15% drop in intent; “I felt guilty when I saw the water number” – Participant 3.

**Key Takeaways**  
1. Water stats resonate better than carbon metrics.  
2. Technical language can overwhelm.

**Decisions & Actions**  
1. Highlight the water badge on product listings.  
2. Simplify carbon info to relatable metrics (“Equivalent to a 20 km drive”).  
3. Add an info link “What do these numbers mean?”
