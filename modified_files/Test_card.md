## Test Card 1: Environmental Impact Badge

### Hypothesis  
“Most eco-conscious shoppers will find the water & carbon badges eye-opening, but it may lower their purchase intent.”

> “Seeing the water badge made me hesitate – I didn’t realize how much water goes into making this shirt.” – User A

### Experiment Design  
Randomly assign 200 users to Version A (badges on) or Version B (no badges); measure purchase-intent before and after browsing.

### Metric  
**Purchase-Intent Delta** = Intent_after − Intent_before (percentage points)

### Success Criteria  
- Statistically significant drop (p < 0.05)  
- Effect size ≥ 5%

### Risks & Mitigations  
- **Risk:** Users misunderstand badge units.  
  **Mitigation:** Add tooltip “Water saved vs. new production” on hover.
