# Interview Responses

## Participant 1

**Question 1:** Which do you prefer, renting or buying new products? Can you tell me the reason?

> “Hmm… I usually buy new stuff because I know exactly who wore it before and it avoids any weird surprises. That said, for a wedding or gala I’d definitely rent—it’s cheaper and I don’t have to store it afterward.”

**Question 2:** If there were a platform where you could rent clothes that others have worn but have been cleaned, would you be willing to try it? Why?

> “Yeah, I’d try if they provide detailed cleaning logs—like detergent info and wash temperature—and maybe a quick video of the garment post-cleaning. Otherwise I’m not risking mystery odors.”

**Question 3:** Would you be willing to participate if you could recycle your old clothes through a website and get rewards (such as points, discounts)?

> “Sure, turning clutter into coupons sounds great. I’d actually dig through the back of my closet if I knew I’d get a decent discount on my next purchase.”

**Question 4:** If you are willing to participate, what kind of rewards would be most attractive to you?

> “Honestly, straight-up percentage off or a BOGO token is perfect—no confusing tiers. Surprise bonus points now and then would keep me checking back.”

**Question 5:** What expectations do you have regarding the process of old clothes recycling? (For instance, door-to-door collection, drop-off points, immediate feedback or something else)

> “I’d want a drop box at the corner shop and an SMS saying ‘We got your bag—thank you!’ within minutes. Quick and simple, no extra steps.”

**Question 6:** How should we handle damage to rented items? Options: Security deposit / Platform insurance / Peer reviews.

> “A small refundable deposit works best—instant and hassle-free. Insurance feels like red tape, and peer reviews can get messy.”

**Question 7:** What concerns would you have about wearing clothes that someone else has worn before? If you have such concerns, what measures do you expect the website to take to enhance your trust?

> “Odors and hidden stains are my worry. If they guarantee hypoallergenic cleaning and show before/after pics, I’d be comfortable.”

**Question 8:** What kind of experience do you expect for using this website? What functions are essential for it?

> “Clean layout, big photos, fast load—plus a chat icon in the corner in case I panic. The fewer clicks, the better.”
