# Learning Cards for Your Minimum Viable Product Hypotheses

## Learning Card 1: Home Dashboard Engagement  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
60% of new users will click a Home Dashboard quick-action within 5 days.

**OBSERVATION**  
55% of new users clicked a quick-action; median time to first click was 60 hours (target was 48 hours).

**LEARNING AND INSIGHTS**  
- Quick-action buttons were too small and labels unclear.  
- Some users did not realize they could scroll to see “Browse Sales” on smaller screens.

**DECISIONS AND ACTIONS**  
- Increase quick-action button size by 15% and use more contrasting colors (e.g., green for “Schedule a Pickup”).  
- Add a subtle pulsing animation on first login to guide users to the quick-action area.  
- Reorder buttons so “Browse Sales” appears in the center for greater visibility.

## Learning Card 2: Sales & Checkout Conversion  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
12% of “Add to Cart” actions will convert to a completed order in the same session.

**OBSERVATION**  
9% conversion rate; 30% drop-off between “Begin Checkout” and payment step.

**LEARNING AND INSIGHTS**  
- The progress bar helped, but users still found payment-entry tedious.  
- Lack of a first-time discount incentive was frequently mentioned.

**DECISIONS AND ACTIONS**  
- Introduce a “10% off your first order” coupon displayed at cart checkout to encourage completion.  
- Simplify payment form by auto-detecting card type and reducing manual typing (auto-tabbing between fields).  
- Add a tooltip next to the coupon field: “Enter code here to save instantly.”

## Learning Card 3: Pickup & Drop-off Flow Adoption  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
25% of page visitors will schedule a pickup or view location details within 10 days.

**OBSERVATION**  
Pickup adoption was 18%; drop-off detail-view rate was 22%.

**LEARNING AND INSIGHTS**  
- Users confused charity bins with recycling centers on the map—icons needed labels.  
- Time-slot grouping improved clarity, but the “Next Available” hint was too subtle.

**DECISIONS AND ACTIONS**  
- Update map legend icons with brief text labels (e.g., “Charity Bin,” “Recycling Center”).  
- Make “Next Available” banner more prominent (bold text, highlighted background).  
- Introduce a modal explaining “Why choose pick-up vs. drop-off?” on first visit.

## Learning Card 4: Profile Completion & Rewards Redemption  
**HYPOTHESIS**  
≥ 75% profile completion within 48 hours; ≥ 18% coupon redemption within 30 days.

**OBSERVATION**  
- Profile completion reached 80% within 48 hours.  
- Redemption rate was 15%; many users missed email reminders.

**LEARNING AND INSIGHTS**  
- Email verification delays (SPAM filtering) slowed some users.  
- Users expected coupons to apply automatically rather than entering codes manually.

**DECISIONS AND ACTIONS**  
- Implement SMS-based fallback for email verification when verification email is not delivered.  
- Auto-apply valid coupons at checkout, showing “Coupon applied: SAVE10” so users see confirmation.  
- Display an in-app banner “You have unredeemed coupons!” on login for users who haven’t redeemed their coupons.
