# Interview Responses

## Participant 1

**Question 1:** When you see that producing this T-shirt requires 3,000 liters of water, how does that impact your willingness to buy it?

> "I knew clothing production was wasteful, but seeing concrete numbers makes me reconsider. Maybe I'd still buy it if I knew I'd wear it for years, but definitely not for a one-time event."

**Question 2:** How should we handle damage to rented items? (Options: Security deposit / Platform insurance / Peer reviews)

> "I think platform insurance would be fairest. Deposits feel a bit old-school, and peer reviews could get biased based on personal preferences rather than actual damage."

**Question 3:** Which feature excited you most? Which felt unnecessary? If you could use just one feature today, which would it be? Why not the others?

> "I'm most excited about the wardrobe analytics - seeing exactly how much I'm wasting would be eye-opening. The social sharing features seem less essential to me personally."

**Question 4:** Which do you prefer, renting or buying new products? Can you tell me the reason?

> "I prefer buying basics that I'll wear often, but renting makes so much sense for special occasion outfits. The environmental impact is definitely a factor in that decision."

**Question 5:** If there were a platform where you could rent clothes that others have worn but have been cleaned, would you be willing to try it? Why?

> "Yes, I’d give it a shot for special events—especially if the site shows how thoroughly items are sanitized and tracks how many wears each piece gets. It feels like a smart way to reduce waste without sacrificing style."

**Question 6:** Would you be willing to participate if you could recycle your old clothes through a website and get rewards (such as points, discounts)?

> "Absolutely. Earning points redeemable for discounts on my next orders would motivate me to actually drop off old items rather than let them sit in the closet."

**Question 7:** What expectations do you have regarding the process of old clothes recycling? (For instance, door-to-door collection, drop-off points, immediate feedback or something else)

> "Door-to-door pickup with a two-hour delivery window would be ideal. I’d also want a confirmation notification showing exactly how much impact my donation made—maybe liters of water saved."

**Question 8:** What kind of experience do you expect for using this website? What functions are essential for it?

> "I’d expect a clean, intuitive dashboard that tracks my carbon and water savings, real-time collection status, and clear sanitation details. Easy navigation and quick load times are a must."
