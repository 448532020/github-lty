
# Test Card 1: Resale/Rental Platform Hypothesis

**HYPOTHESIS**  
We believe that 60% of trend-focused users will rent clothes if the rental is affordable.

**TEST**  
To verify that, we will promote the resale/rental platform to trend-focused users through targeted marketing channels. Track the number of rental requests received from this group.

**METRIC**  
And measure the percentage of trend-focused users who submit rental requests.

**CRITERIA**  
We are right if at least 60% of the targeted trend-focused users submit rental requests.

# Test Card 2: Fast Fashion Impact Awareness Hypothesis

**HYPOTHESIS**  
We believe that showing environmental costs (e.g., CO₂/water) reduces purchase intent.

**TEST**  
To verify that, we will select a group of potential fast-fashion buyers. Show them the static webpage with environmental cost data after they express an initial interest in buying fast-fashion items. Compare their purchase intent before and after viewing the page.

**METRIC**  
And measure the change in the number of users who say they will reduce or stop their fast-fashion purchases.

**CRITERIA**  
We are right if there is a significant decrease in the number of users with purchase intent after viewing the page.

# Test Card 4: Clothing Pickup Service Hypothesis

**HYPOTHESIS**  
We believe that people forget to donate clothes, and a monthly pickup service with tracking notifications will increase donation rates.

**TEST**  
To verify that, we will launch the clothing pickup service in a test area. Compare the number of clothes donations before and after the service launch. Also, track user engagement with the tracking notifications.

**METRIC**  
And measure the increase in the number of clothes donations and the open rate of tracking notifications.

**CRITERIA**  
We are right if there is a notable increase in donations and a reasonable open rate for notifications.

# Test Card 5: Thrift Store/Op Shop Website Hypothesis

**HYPOTHESIS**  
We believe that a website that showcases used clothes on models and provides an informative page will increase the purchase rate of second-hand clothes.

**TEST**  
To verify that, we will launch the website and compare the purchase rates of second-hand clothes before and after the website launch. Also, collect user feedback on the website's features.

**METRIC**  
And measure the increase in the number of second-hand clothes sold and user satisfaction scores.

**CRITERIA**  
We are right if there is a significant increase in sales and positive user feedback.
