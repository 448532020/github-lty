# Competitor Analysis

| Competitor         | Description                                                     | Key Features                                                             | Pricing Model                                   |
|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| **Rent the Runway**| A subscription-based clothing rental service offering designer pieces for special occasions or everyday wear. | • Unlimited swaps per month<br>• Access to high-end designer inventory<br>• Built-in styling recommendations | Monthly subscription tiers (e.g. 4 items/month, 8 items/month) |
| **ThredUp**        | An online consignment and thrift store for buying and selling second-hand clothing. | • Mail-in “Clean Out” kits<br>• Filtered search by brand, size, condition<br>• Seller payout or donation options | Buy-as-you-go pricing; seller commission varies (5–80%) |
| **Depop**          | A mobile-first peer-to-peer marketplace for vintage and streetwear. | • Social-feed style listings<br>• In-app messaging buyer↔seller<br>• Global shipping integration | Listing fee + 10% transaction fee               |
| **Vinted**         | A peer-to-peer marketplace for everyday second-hand fashion with no seller fees in many markets. | • Free listings (in many regions)<br>• Buyer covers shipping fees<br>• Bundle discount feature | Buyer-paid shipping; no seller commission       |
| **The RealReal**   | A luxury consignment marketplace specializing in authenticated designer resale. | • Professional authentication<br>• White-glove pickup & delivery (select markets)<br>• In-app bidding/“Make An Offer” | Consignor split (20–50% of sale price)          |
