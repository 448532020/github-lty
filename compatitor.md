## Insights & Positioning

1. **Differentiation**  
   - Unlike niche players, our MVP unifies rentals, sales, recycling and impact tracking in a single UX.  
   - Built-in rewards and gamification create stronger habit formation than pure resale platforms.

2. **Opportunities**  
   - **Partnerships** with local charities/logistics providers to reduce shipping costs and carbon footprint.  
   - **Data insights** product for B2B: aggregated sustainability metrics for brands and municipalities.

3. **Challenges**  
   - **Inventory depth**: competing with well-established landlords of designer and thrift inventory.  
   - **Trust & quality**: must implement authentication, clear condition grading, and reliable logistics to match incumbents.

4. **Strategic Next Steps**  
   - **Pilot launch** in a single metropolitan area to refine pickup/drop-off logistics.  
   - **User acquisition** via sustainability influencers and campus ambassador programs.  
   - **Iterate** rapidly on Test & Learning cards for core flows (dashboard engagement, checkout conversion, recycling/pickup adoption).
