
# Business Model Canvas

## Customer Segments

- **Trend-focused Consumers**  
  People who follow fashion trends but are also conscious of cost and sustainability.  
  *Accepted hypothesis:* They are likely to rent clothes if the price is affordable.

- **Environmentally-conscious Shoppers**  
  Those who are concerned about the environmental impact of fast fashion.  
  *Accepted hypothesis:* Showing environmental costs can reduce their purchase intent.

- **Busy Individuals**  
  People who want to donate clothes but often forget due to their busy schedules.  
  *Accepted hypothesis:* A convenient pickup service will encourage them to donate.

- **Budget-conscious Shoppers**  
  Looking for affordable clothing options.  
  *Accepted hypothesis:* A thrift store website can attract them.

---

## Value Propositions

- **Resale/Rental Platform**  
  Provide affordable and trendy clothing rental options for trend-focused consumers, reducing the need for new purchases.

- **Fast Fashion Impact Awareness Page**  
  Educate environmentally-conscious shoppers about the environmental costs of fast fashion, influencing their buying decisions.

- **Clothing Pickup Service**  
  Offer a hassle-free way for busy individuals to donate clothes, increasing donation rates.

- **Thrift Store/Op Shop Website**  
  Provide a wide range of affordable second-hand clothes for budget-conscious shoppers.

---

## Channels

- **Online Marketing**  
  Use social media, email marketing, and search engine optimization to reach potential customers.

- **Partnerships**  
  Collaborate with fashion influencers, environmental organizations, and local community groups.

- **Word-of-Mouth**  
  Encourage satisfied customers to spread the word about the services.

---

## Customer Relationships

- **Personalized Service**  
  Manually match rental requests on the resale/rental platform and provide personalized email responses for the recycling drop-off map.

- **Notification System**  
  Keep customers informed about pickup services and new arrivals on the thrift store website.

- **Feedback Collection**  
  Regularly collect feedback from customers to improve the services.
